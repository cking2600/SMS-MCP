Privacy Policy

Skynet Alerts, operated by Christopher King. Effective October 12, 2026.

What we collect

For each person who agrees to receive Skynet Alerts: their name, the account username the alerts are about, their mobile phone number, and a record of their consent. For each message sent: the time, the text, who sent it, and the delivery status reported by the carrier.

How we use it

Only to send account security alerts to the person the information belongs to, and to keep a record of what was sent. It is not used for marketing, advertising, or any other purpose.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Phone numbers and message content are passed only to the text messaging provider (Twilio) for the sole purpose of delivering the message. We do not sell any personal information.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving them. Your number will not be texted again unless you agree again.

Security and retention

Information is stored on access-controlled systems and kept only as long as needed to provide the alerts and keep a record of them.
