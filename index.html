Skynet Alerts

Account security text messages from Skynet, a security operations tool built and operated by Christopher King.

What this program is

Skynet watches for security events on the accounts and computers it monitors. When something needs a person's attention, such as an account locked after repeated failed sign-ins or a sign-in from an unusual location, a security analyst can send that person a short text message about it. Messages are sent one at a time by a person, never in bulk, and never contain marketing or promotional content.

Who receives messages

Only people who have agreed to receive these alerts, at the mobile number they provided for account security. Skynet cannot text a number that was not provided this way.

How to sign up

Before a number is ever texted, the operator asks the person, in conversation, whether they agree to receive account security text messages from Skynet Alerts at that number, and explains that message and data rates may apply and that they can reply STOP at any time. The number is used only after the person says yes. See the consent script.

How often

Occasional. Messages are sent only when there is a security matter that needs attention, and never more than 5 per hour to one person.

Stopping and help

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving them. Reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.
