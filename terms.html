Program

Skynet Alerts sends account security text messages, such as notice of a locked account or an unusual sign-in, to people who have agreed to receive them. Messages are sent one at a time by a security analyst and contain no marketing.

Message frequency varies. Messages are sent only when there is a security matter that needs attention, and never more than 5 per hour to one person.
